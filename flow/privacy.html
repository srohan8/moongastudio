App Privacy Policy

Flow  ·  Effective date: March 2026  ·  Plain English, by design.

Flow is built for people who deserve a quiet, safe space to work. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data to anyone. This document explains exactly what we store, why, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Who we are

Flow is published by Moonga. Questions about this policy: [email protected]

What we collect

We collect the minimum possible to make the app work.

On your device (local only):

  • Your tasks and energy check-ins
  • Your sound and timer preferences
  • The last room you visited and your intention

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it.

On our server (Supabase):

  • A random device ID generated the first time you open the app — not linked to any account, email, or identity
  • Your chosen nickname (e.g. "Quiet Fox") — you pick this, it can be anything
  • Which room you're in and your intention, while your session is active
  • Chat messages you send in a room
  • Private room settings you create

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any account credentials. There are no accounts in Flow.

How long we keep it

  • Session presence — deleted automatically when you leave a room, or after 2 minutes of inactivity
  • Chat messages — deleted automatically after 24 hours
  • Private rooms — kept until you delete them
  • Visitor counts — rolling 24-hour anonymised counts, no individual records

Analytics

We use PostHog for basic analytics — how many people use the app, which rooms are most visited, and where people drop off. Analytics are anonymised. No personal data is involved.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — our database provider. Their privacy policy.
  • PostHog — anonymised analytics. Their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts load from Google's CDN. Google may log this request.

We do not use advertising networks, social trackers, or any data brokers.

Children

Flow is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will delete any associated data promptly.

Your rights

  • Delete your data — go to the You tab in the app and tap "Clear all data"
  • Request server-side deletion — email us with your device ID (found in the You tab) and we'll remove all records within 7 days
  • Ask what we have — same email, same 7-day turnaround

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We won't use your data in new ways without telling you first.

Questions or deletion requests

[email protected]

We'll respond within 7 days.
