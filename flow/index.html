What is Flow

Belong somewhere.
Get things done there.

Flow is not a productivity tool. It's a third place — a virtual café where you settle in, see who else is around, and quietly get things done together.

For people whose brains work differently, being alone feels hard and loud spaces feel impossible. Flow is the in-between: ambient, low-pressure, and genuinely kind.

Gentle Task List

No streaks. No guilt. No gamification. Just your tasks, when you're ready for them.
