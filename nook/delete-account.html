Delete your account

Nook · by Moonga Studio

Delete from inside the app

The quickest way to delete your account and all associated data is directly in Nook:

  1. Open Nook and tap the You tab
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm when prompted

Your account, direct messages, and friend connections are deleted immediately.

Request deletion by email

Email hello@moonga.studio with the subject Account deletion request — Nook. Include the email address associated with your account. We'll confirm deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Data When deleted
Account (email + password) Immediately on deletion
Direct messages Immediately on deletion
Friend connections Immediately on deletion
Private rooms you created Immediately on deletion
Room chat messages Automatically after 24 hours (no account needed)
Local data (nickname, tasks, sound preference) Stored on your device only — clear via your phone's app storage settings
Anonymous analytics No personal identifiers — cannot be linked back to you

Public room sessions are anonymous and are not linked to your account. They expire automatically.
