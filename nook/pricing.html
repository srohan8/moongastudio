Questions

Do I need to sign up to use Nook?

No. You can enter any public room without an account. Creating an account (free) lets you save your task list, add friends, and send room invites.

What's a founding member?

The first 1,000 people to join Nook. Founding members get private room hosting included free for life — even after we introduce paid hosting for new members. This offer won't come back once the spots are filled.

How do private rooms work?

You create a room, choose a scene, sound, and timer setup, then share a short code with your friends. Anyone with the code can join. The room is yours — strangers can't wander in.

Can I cancel hosting anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. Cancel and your private room converts — it stays visible to existing members but won't accept new joins.

Is Nook available on Android?

Yes — Nook is on Android via the Play Store, and also runs in any browser. The experience is the same across both.
