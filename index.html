Moonga is an independent studio publishing under a single vision: make things that matter to real people. Our apps are designed around human needs. Our games are rooted in stories that deserve to be told.

We're based in Kerala, India — and proud of it. The folklore, the colour, the texture of this place lives in everything we make.
